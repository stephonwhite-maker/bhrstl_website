Let Us Streamline Your Patient Intakes & Assessments.

Increase your staff productivity while we provide telehealth patient intakes and assessments. We're available 24/7 to complete assessments, including initial intake and annual re-evaluation for continuation of services.

  • Increase billable hours for in-house staff.

Improve patient care and reduce wait times with 24/7 on-demand level of care assessments. Our telehealth emergency department services include real-time behavioral health assessments, collaborative crisis response and safety planning, continuous bed placement services, and follow-up case management upon discharge.

  • Increase inpatient diversion.
  • Reduce facility risks.
  • Decrease patient return visits.

Why choose BHR?

BHR stands out for its compassionate care, proven expertise, and 24/7 commitment to those in need. We deliver timely, effective, and person-centered solutions that make a lasting impact. Choose BHR for trusted support, innovation, and excellence in behavioral health care.

  • We're Always Here

    Our team is available 24/7, which means greater patient service capacity and reduced wait times.

  • We're Trained and Experienced

    We only employ trauma-informed, qualified mental health clinicians. We train, credential, and supervise our clinicians to ensure excellence in patient diagnoses and treatment recommendations.

  • We Know Healthcare Providers

    We develop specialized training to ensure our clinicians are versed in your specific electronic health record system, assessment practices, facility-offered services, and surrounding community referrals.

  • We're Efficient and Proficient

    We strive to maintain quick response times and immediate documentation. We complete paperwork in real time and complete intake services within 24 hours for fast patient turnaround and access to services.

  • We Think Globally

    We continuously review the development of new regulations, technological advances, and best practices within telehealth, ensuring your services are secure, compliant, and cutting edge.
